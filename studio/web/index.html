New project

A project is one brand mark: its images, groups and snapshots are its own. The mark sits at the same place in every shot, and every image is checked for it.

A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best (a mark on a plain background works too).

Demo keeps its mark, its size and place and the words its prompts use for it (every image is checked against them). For another mark or place, make a new project.
