# AGENTS.md

**Phosphene**: a logo-animation film generator (the name since 2026-10-26; it was "Demo Studio", and it is meant to go beyond the Demo logo). 9:16 match-cut films where every shot places the brand's mark at the same place and size, in a different material/scene. **Projects** (one per mark, `studio/projects.py`): Demo ("demo") is the first, its circular orbital logo at exact dead-center (50%, 50%), 18% of frame width.

**Words**: not about brands either (a mark can be a brand's logo, that's all): the project form's one line is **About** ("Who or what these films are for: a brand, a project, an event…", still `brief` in the files), and Kimi's prompts say mark / project, not brand. A mark 30% of the width or more (`BIG_MARK`) is "one large" in Kimi's request, which asks for subjects that can carry it at that size (a wall, a panel, a sheet, a screen...), never a small object it would shrink onto: Halo's 50% ring was "one small", and urchins, tampers, zip pulls and matchboxes came back with the mark shrunk, trashed by the check (2026-10-29, from the user's curation). **Words**: the app says **mark**, never logo (the user's call, 2026-10-29: it was made with logos but is for marks and shapes of all sorts): the mark check (a card's "Mark check" badge, `trash_reason`), the mark target (T), the check's messages. Code names (`qa_engine`, `toggleReticle`, "logo" in comments) stay.

## Projects (studio/projects.py)
A project is a folder `projects/<slug>/` with a `project.json`: name, the mark (`mark.png`, any shape: the page draws an SVG or any image into a PNG trimmed to the mark before sending it), `mark_size` (% of the width), `mark_x` / `mark_y` (% across / down), `mark_shape` and `mark_noun` (how the prompts describe it), `brief` (who it is for, one line: it replaces "Demo, a creative toolkit for creatives at an agency group" in the quality bar), `palette` (hex), and where its plates, curation, caches and guide images live (`paths`; by default all inside its folder). New projects are gitignored (`projects/*`, they stay on the machine that made them); `projects/demo/project.json` is committed and points at DEMO's old places (assets/ai-plates, studio/curation_state.json, assets/logo-guides, ...), so nothing of DEMO moved. The server works on one project at a time: the open one is remembered in `studio/.local.json` (gitignored); `apply_project` points `PLATES_DIR`, `CURATION_FILE`, `QA_CACHE_FILE`, `THUMBS_DIR` and the features cache at it (`/api/projects`: list, switch, create, update; refused with 409 while something runs; the page reloads after a switch). Everything brand-specific reads the project: the prompt sentence (`build_full_prompt`, `mark_words`: for DEMO exactly the old words, a test guards them), the quality bar (`quality_bar`), world invention, softening, DEMO's colours in the worlds and colour ideas mapped to the project's palette (`projects.rebrand`, DEMO unchanged), the Krea guide (made from the mark at its size and place: cream on near-black and ink on cream, `make_guides`; uploaded once, `guide_cache_file`, forgotten when the mark changes), the logo check (`qa_engine.mark_spec`: the project's mark at its size and place; DEMO keeps `DEMO_MARK`, its orbital mask and its tuned check, unchanged; a test showed it gives identical results on 40 real plates. Another mark has its own check, `_check_project_mark`, with two outcomes, tuned on the user's own keep / trash of Nike, a triangle (Triad Noir, 47.5% wide), McDonald's and Kestrel: on simple solid shapes no overlap measure tells "right size" from "a bit too big" for sure (a kept triangle and a trashed one scored the same), so only the clearly wrong is trashed (fit at its size under 0.33, or a size 1.4x+ / 0.7x- fitting clearly better), and the doubtful (fit under 0.42, the best size off by more than ~12%, or no sharper at its size than a fifth smaller or bigger; or faint, a fit of 0.2-0.33, but peaking sharply at its own size, 0.15+ over 0.8x and 1.2x: `OWN_FAINT`, from Halo's scrim and blind emboss the user restored, 2026-10-29) stays in the selection with a "Check the mark" badge (`qa_doubt`, amber; the reason in Info). The fit is the flat mark either way round or its outline against the image's edges (an emboss), a little off its place included, at 15 sizes (`normalized_corr` sums in C, the mark's own sums once a size, `corr_ref`: the same numbers as the old pixel loop, 3-4x faster; ~0.7 s an image at 18% wide, ~6 s at 40%, ~10-13 s at 50%). A new image is checked before it joins the plates and outside `STATE_LOCK` (`check_new_image`, its result cached under its final name): checked inside it, every Trash / Restore click during a batch waited for it (37-49 s an image on Halo's 50% mark). ## Safety (from a review of the app branch)
- Guardrails (from a second review, of jobs against actions): the curation (`state.json`) is written whole or not at all (`_write_atomic`: a temp file, then `os.replace`), and one that cannot be read is set aside as `*.damaged-<time>.json`, never written over (a half-read file used to be replaced by an empty curation). Every read-change-write of it holds `STATE_LOCK` (trash, Settings, a new image's entry); a slow call (Kimi writing scenes, the key check) works from a copy and merges into the state as it is then, only in the project it started in (`started_in`). New images are written whole (`.part`, then renamed) before they appear. A job's requests say their project (`X-Phosphene-Project`, `forProject`): a request for a project no longer open is refused (409 `project_changed`); a page whose project was changed elsewhere reloads into it (`loadState`). While a batch or an export runs the page holds a lease (`POST /api/busy`, `_LEASE`, renewed every 10 s, at most 60 s): `busy()` stays true in the gaps, so no switch, New, import or delete from another window or the Mac menu, and `dev.py` never restarts mid-job (it also waits when /api/busy does not answer). The page's own files likewise: while a job runs, a reload from the development server (an edit to the page) is held (`src/devguard.js`, `vite:beforeFullReload`) and done once the job ends: an edit mid-batch had reloaded the page, the images in flight lost it, and Resume asked for them twice. Selection / Trash switch at once from what the page has (`renderView`), then refresh. Files opened from the Mac menu or Finder while the app runs go through the page (`page_import` → `importProjectPath`, which waits for a job); a page not ready yet (loading, reloading) hands the path back (`import-later`), kept in `Opened` and given to the page when it says it is ready (`page-ready`, after its first load): it used to reload and drop the file; New project / Project settings (⌘N too) wait; Empty trash and Delete selected wait for an export (it reads the images as it goes). Export project… reads the curation under the lock.
- Image names become paths only through `plate_name` (server) and `_clean_state` (a received project's curation): a bare file name, never `../` or an absolute path, so Empty trash can never delete outside the project's plates (a crafted .phosphene listing `/Users/.../thesis.pdf` as trashed did, before).
- The server answers only requests to `localhost` / `127.0.0.1` by that name (`from_this_computer`: a domain pointed at 127.0.0.1, DNS rebinding, is refused) and takes commands (POST) only from its own page (an `Origin` that is not local is refused: another website cannot empty the trash or switch project). Clients with no Origin (the app shell, curl, tests) are local by construction.
- The static fallback serves only `/assets/` (the old plate URLs), never a dotfile: `studio/.secrets.json` and `.krea_auth.json` are not reachable.
- A damaged project never stops the server: `open_first_project` passes it over (the next project, else the first screen). Imports: numeric names are made text, Finder's `__MACOSX`/`._` entries are ignored, a damaged zip says so and leaves nothing behind, two imports at once cannot delete each other's folder. Switching project holds `STATE_LOCK`. Krea token refreshes are one at a time (`_REFRESH_LOCK`). In the app no folder is made inside Phosphene.app (it may run read-only from the DMG).
- The app's port is fixed (47615) when free: the Krea sign-in comes back to the same address and the page's remembered settings (tied to the address) survive a relaunch. The menu bar: About Phosphene (the icon, the version, one line on what it is: `AboutMetadata`), New Project ⌘N, Open Project File… ⌘O (the macOS file dialog), Export Project… ⌘E, Curate ⌘1, Film ⌘2, Settings ⌘,, the standard Edit menu (the page gets every shortcut first: its own ⌘Z / ⌘S / ⌘A are not doubled). Files opened from Finder are imported off the main thread.

## Sound engine performance (studio/web/src/drone.js)
The live drone must never make the page janky. What keeps it cheap, and what tests guard (`studio/tests/e2e/drone.e2e.test.cjs`): cut-time events, hits and arpeggio notes are scheduled ~0.8 s ahead (`advance`, ticked every 90 ms), not for the whole clip; the arpeggio and each hit type are fixed voices retriggered by automation (no node per note); the reverb convolver is shared across swaps (loading its impulse response is the costliest step); noise and room sample data are made once per sample rate and shared by every context; settings changes are coalesced into one swap per ~120 ms, a swap whose only difference is where the clip ends (the Multiple) is skipped, and the exact loudness is measured when the page is idle. A new sound starts a moment ahead (the time a build takes), and its fade-in and the old sound's fade-out are timed from that instant: a fade timed before the build made every swap enter with a step. The room's impulse is noise band-limited to 150-900 Hz: low-passed noise alone drifts, and for some seeds made the room a strong boost near 100 Hz that swamped the sub (a test renders 20 seeds with a big room). Measured on a 300-cut fast clip with every layer on: a swap ~12 ms, longest main-thread block ~45 ms (it was 675 ms). 2026-10-30 (the user felt fatigue in the UI): the reverb rooms no longer depend on the seed (`ROOM_SEED`: a new room per Random cost up to 100 ms), the live sound's rooms are made in a worker (`roomInWorker`; the Hall's 8 s room took 60-80 ms, dragging Space stalled; until ready the nearest room it has plays), the level reading takes a 1.5 s room of the same energy (`quickRoom`), slow curves have at most 600 points (`sampled`), and the audio context is made at the first idle moment even with the sound off (the first Random froze ~115 ms). Measured with every layer and the heaviest kinds on (moving pulse, Glass, Hall, Euclid): playing, dragging sliders, the timeline, Random and Random sound while playing: 60 fps, no main-thread block; the test asks for 60 fps and no block over 60 ms. The Pad's tone (`PAD_TONES`: Warm as before, Glass each note folded from triangles with a glassy top, Hollow a slowly sweeping band-pass) and the Sub's fourth wave, Pulse (the saw minus itself a moment later, the width breathing 36-64%), came with it (2026-10-30, variety on the same patch). WebKit (Safari, the Mac app) can stop a page's sound by itself while it plays (`interrupted` / `suspended`: another app's sound, an output change, sleep) and never start it again: `Live` asks it back at once (`onstatechange`, `_revive`), and again on focus, a click or a key, then the page puts it where the picture is (`onRevived` → `seek`). In the Mac app the sound also went silent after the window had been behind another app while the context still said `running`: in WebKit (`renewOnReturn`, Apple's `navigator.vendor`; Chrome keeps its sound), back in front while playing, `renew` closes the context and the page plays a fresh one from the picture's place (`onRenew` → `startMusic`; ~0.5 s gap). The console log (right-click › Inspect Element in `app:live`) showed worse: after a clack, every context, new ones too, said `running` and stayed silent; only a reload helped. So a watchdog (`_watch`, never a reload while a job runs: a fresh sound instead) checks every second, while the page is in front, that the sound's clock (`currentTime`) moves; two stopped seconds call `onStall`: a fresh sound, and stopped again within 20 s, a reload that plays on from the same place (`studioResume`). Every event is logged as `[sound hh:mm:ss]` (`soundLog`). The app also has `NSAppSleepDisabled` (app/src-tauri/Info.plist: no App Nap). The cause, found in another Tauri app on macOS 27 (thecrateapp/crate#270): hidden ~4 minutes, WKWebView suspends the page's process and deactivates its audio session; the context still says `running`, the output is dead until a reload. The window has `disable_drag_drop_handler()`: Tauri's file-drop handler took every drag, so cards could not be reordered in the app (the page's drag test passes in Chrome and WebKit; only the shell broke it; README's checklist has a drag). The window is made with `background_throttling(Disabled)` (WKInactiveSchedulingPolicy::None, macOS 14+): never suspended (timers keep their pace while hidden). `renewOnReturn` is off: rebuilding on return did not mend it and added a gap. By hand, the reset is Sound › Source Off then Synthesized (`updateMusic` → `Live.fresh`: a new context), no extra button (one control per feature); its tooltip says so. No step on the cuts (2026-10-30, the user heard "tick tick tick here and there" and took it for a defect): the pump came back up and the cut's tone started in one sample, and an instant step of a 50 Hz sine is a click, loud or not by where the wave was (measured: the top at a cut ~800x the rest at Pulse 0.9, 47x from cut to cut); both now rise over a few ms (`RISE` 4 ms, `TICK_RISE` 2 ms, time constants): ~1.2x, and a test renders Pulse 0 to 1 and checks it. `renderExport` / `level` / `Live` accept any numbers (empty or one-cut timelines, NaN, huge offsets) without throwing.
