# AGENTS.md

**Phosphene**: a logo-animation film generator (the name since 2026-10-26; it was "Demo Studio", and it is meant to go beyond the Demo logo). 9:16 match-cut films where every shot places the brand's mark at the same place and size, in a different material/scene. **Projects** (one per mark, `studio/projects.py`): Demo ("demo") is the first, its circular orbital logo at exact dead-center (50%, 50%), 18% of frame width.

## Projects (studio/projects.py)
A project is a folder `projects/<slug>/` with a `project.json`: name, the mark (`mark.png`, any shape: the page draws an SVG or any image into a PNG trimmed to the mark before sending it), `mark_size` (% of the width), `mark_x` / `mark_y` (% across / down), `mark_shape` and `mark_noun` (how the prompts describe it), `brief` (who it is for, one line: it replaces "Demo, a creative toolkit for creatives at an agency group" in the quality bar), `palette` (hex), and where its plates, curation, caches and guide images live (`paths`; by default all inside its folder). New projects are gitignored (`projects/*`, they stay on the machine that made them); `projects/demo/project.json` is committed and points at DEMO's old places (assets/ai-plates, studio/curation_state.json, assets/logo-guides, ...), so nothing of DEMO moved. The server works on one project at a time: the open one is remembered in `studio/.local.json` (gitignored); `apply_project` points `PLATES_DIR`, `CURATION_FILE`, `QA_CACHE_FILE`, `THUMBS_DIR` and the features cache at it (`/api/projects`: list, switch, create, update; refused with 409 while something runs; the page reloads after a switch). Everything brand-specific reads the project: the prompt sentence (`build_full_prompt`, `mark_words`: for DEMO exactly the old words, a test guards them), the quality bar (`quality_bar`), world invention, softening and the critic's rubric, DEMO's colours in the worlds and colour ideas mapped to the project's palette (`projects.rebrand`, DEMO unchanged), the Krea guide (made from the mark at its size and place: cream on near-black and ink on cream, `make_guides`; uploaded once, `guide_cache_file`, forgotten when the mark changes), the logo check (`qa_engine.mark_spec`: the project's mark at its size and place; DEMO keeps `DEMO_MARK`, its orbital mask and its tuned check, unchanged; a test showed it gives identical results on 40 real plates. Another mark has its own check, `_check_project_mark`, with two outcomes, tuned on the user's own keep / trash of Nike, a triangle (Triad Noir, 47.5% wide), McDonald's and Kestrel: on simple solid shapes no overlap measure tells "right size" from "a bit too big" for sure (a kept triangle and a trashed one scored the same), so only the clearly wrong is trashed (fit at its size under 0.33, or a size 1.4x+ / 0.7x- fitting clearly better), and the doubtful (fit under 0.42, the best size off by more than ~12%, or no sharper at its size than a fifth smaller or bigger) stays in the selection with a "Check the mark" badge (`qa_doubt`, amber; the reason in Info). The fit is the flat mark either way round or its outline against the image's edges (an emboss), a little off its place included, at 15 sizes (`normalized_corr` sums in C, the mark's own sums once a size, `corr_ref`: the same numbers as the old pixel loop, 3-4x faster; ~0.7 s an image at 18% wide, ~6 s at 40%, ~10-13 s at 50%). A new image is checked before it joins the plates and outside `STATE_LOCK` (`check_new_image`, its result cached under its final name): checked inside it, every Trash / Restore click during a batch waited for it (37-49 s an image on Halo's 50% mark). Replayed on the user's 33 judged images: no keep trashed, 8 of 9 trashes trashed or noted; on synthetic circle, square, ring, wordmark, monogram, triangle and arches: right size (flat or embossed) always passes, 0.8x / 1.25x noted or trashed, 0.6x / 1.8x trashed. `OWN_CHECK_VERSION` is in the QA cache's keys so older results are measured again; **`npm run qa:replay`** (`studio/qa_replay.py`) measures every image of the app's projects again (~/Documents/Phosphene, DEMO left out) and lists where the check and the user's curation disagree (kept or restored but trashed by the check; in the Trash but passed); `--save` before a change to the check and `--against` after it list what the change flipped: the regression check, run before and after every change to the check. The fixed set (the user's idea: invented shapes, no brand): `studio/tests/qa_shapes.py` draws 11 marks (letters R / AK / VOLT; ring, holed triangle, star, chevron, notched square; three rings, flower, dot grid; 20-45% wide) into 17 scenes each with the answer known (flat, light on dark, embossed, faint, a little off, dark on dark, lit from one side, busy texture, a shadow across, shaded: never trashed; 0.6x, 1.8x, moved, missing: trashed; 0.8x, 1.25x, a disc in its place: flagged); `test_qa_shapes.py` runs it only when the check's code changed (a fingerprint, ~85 s), with today's misses in `KNOWN`. Synthetic scenes stay easier than generated images, so the subtle end needs real judged images (invented marks as projects, judged by the user: not built yet). 2026-10-28: 64 of 69 agree. The user does not want a perfect check (there will always be manual curation): after each round of logos they say so, and the check is reviewed on their new verdicts, without regressions), the logo target in the page (`--mark-w/x/y/ar`, `showProject`: the mark's box), full-size plates (`/api/plate`) and the export's name. DEMO is fixed (its mark, size and place; "Project settings…" is off for it): another DEMO with other settings is a new project. Where things live: `studio/places.py` (the repo's places, or with `PHOSPHENE_HOME` / `PHOSPHENE_SUPPORT` / `PHOSPHENE_WEB` / `PHOSPHENE_FFMPEG` the Mac app's: projects in ~/Documents/Phosphene, this machine's files (keys, the Krea sign-in, the log, exports) in ~/Library/Application Support/Phosphene). The app ships no project and copies none in (Demo travels as a .phos, Export project…): `seed_examples` and `PHOSPHENE_EXAMPLES` are left from when it did and are not set by the app. **Export project…** (`export_zip`: NAME.phos, one zip with the project's folder: project.json without paths, mark, guides, state.json with the groups, snapshots, order and trash, every image, and what was measured (`qa.json`, `features.json`: the logo check and the looks, only for its images; without them an import measured every image again, ~1.5 s each, before the page opened); never the Kimi key; downloaded once through `/api/download-zip`) and **Import project…** (`import_zip`: always a new project, its settings checked as New project checks them and its curation cleaned (bare text names only, never a Kimi key: `_clean_state`); if a job started while it was copied it is imported but not opened (`imported_opens`), named "Nike 2" when a "Nike" is already here (as Finder numbers a copy), only a project's files, nothing outside its folder; then it opens). A new project starts with the mark (the first field; everything else and Create are off until it is read, `formStage` pick / reading / ready; Cancel always works and drops the answer): it is read (`/api/describe-mark`): its own colours from its pixels (`mark_colours`) and Kimi's name for it, a line on the brand, its shape and what to call it and colours to go with it (`describe_mark`, one try of at most 60 s); a spinner and the seconds show while Kimi reads; then everything can be changed (never written over what was typed). The preview's ground is mid grey so a black mark and a white one both show, and the mark has the logo target's dashed box around it, as every image will. While images are made (or an AI order, an export or a purge runs: `isBusy`) the project stays: the page drives that work and a switch reloads it, so the menu greys out the other projects, New, Import and Delete and says why (`gm-note`; Export stays), and the server refuses a switch (409). Opening another project shows a cover (`cover`), then the loader names it. The library header (after Claude Design): Selection | Trash with their counts; "AI order…" (hidden in the Trash); Download, the logo target and Refresh in one bordered group (`.tool-group`); the size slider. In the Trash: "Pick images to restore or delete" and Select all on the left, Restore (lit once something is picked), Delete N… (only then) and Empty trash… on the right. Curate's **Download** (`downloadShown`, `/api/download-zip`) zips the images shown (All, the group, or the Trash), full size, in order. Header: the project's mark and name (a dark mark shows as a light silhouette there and in the menu, `fitMarkTone`: measured from its opaque pixels, under 0.3 luminance), a menu to switch, New project…, Project settings…, Export project…, Import project… Delete project… (`deleteProject`, asked first with its image count; `projects.trash`: its whole folder to the macOS Trash, `TRASH_DIR`, as "Nike (Phosphene project)", recoverable until emptied; refused while something runs and for a project whose files live outside its folder, the repository's Demo; the open one gone, the next opens, or none) (the form `#projectModal`: name, mark, size, across, down, its shape, called, brand, colours, with a 9:16 preview).


## Targets (studio/placement.py)
Where each image's mark goes (levels, at the top of placement.py: `LEVELS`, `SIZE_PCT`, `MARGIN`; the user's call 2026-10-29: levels in the target menu at generation time, not numbers in Project settings), chosen per batch in the generation row (`#targetSelect`, between Refine and the count, remembered per project, locked while a batch runs, sent with every image as `target` so a Resume keeps it). **On target** (`exact`, the default): the project's place and size, as always; no placement is stored and nothing about it changed (DEMO's words, guides and check included). **Loose**: each image gets its own place and size before its scene is written (`invent-prompts` with `target`: `_ask_chunk` chooses one per shot and gives it to Kimi in that shot's line, "the mark: exact point (x% across, y% down), ~size% frame width", and tells it the mark is often not at the centre, so the scene is composed around it; the idea carries its `placement` to `/api/generate-image`, which uses it (checked by `placement.clean`) or chooses one if none came) (`placement.choose`, bands that never overlap so each level reads as itself and never as On target (the user's call, 2026-10-29: Loose close to the primary target, Scattered all over, and the size visibly different): Loose 5-12% of the frame away from the project's place (the larger of across and down), 0.75-0.9x or 1.12-1.3x its size; **Scattered** 18% or more away, 0.5-0.8x or 1.25-1.8x; both within 6-45% of the width, the box 6% clear of the frame's edges), draws that image's guide there (`placement.guide`, in a temp folder, uploaded per image), names it in the prompt (`build_full_prompt(raw, placement.as_project(...))`: a mark away from the centre is said FIRST and in words, "COMPOSITION: the mark sits in the upper-left area of the frame, NOT at the centre (x% from the left and y% from the top, ~size% of the width)... compose the scene around that spot" (`frame_area`), then the scene, then "EXACT POINT x% from the left and y% from the top": the first Scattered images, a spot given only in numbers at the end, were pulled to the centre; On target's words unchanged), checks the logo there (`current_mark(pl)`: the project's mark at that size and place, DEMO's too through the general check; cache keys `...:at:size:x:y`), tells the critic, and keeps it in the curation (`st['placements'][filename]`, cleaned on import: `placement.clean`). `/api/state` gives each plate its `placement`; the logo target draws each card's box there (`placeVars` on `.card-thumb`) and the Film's box follows the image shown (`shownBox`); the crosshair stays at the project's place, as the reference. Next (the agreed plan, 2026-10-29): Angled (turned / seen from the side: the guide in perspective), Free (no guide; a check for presence), a Target group (several kinds at once), then, after looking at the images, AI order reading placements and Film's stabilise / motion lane.

## Layout
- `studio/server.py` — the API server (port 8765, `PORT` env to change): JSON API, plates and exported video. It also serves the built page from `studio/web/dist` if `npm run build` was run.
- `studio/web/` — the frontend (Vite, vanilla JS): `index.html` (markup), `src/app.js`, `src/style.css`, `vite.config.js` (proxies `/api`, `/assets`, `/outputs` to :8765). Inline `onclick=` handlers need the function exported at the bottom of `app.js` (a test checks this).
- `studio/krea_mcp_client.py` — Krea image generation over MCP (`https://api.krea.ai/mcp`). The app signs in to Krea itself, as an MCP client (OAuth 2.1: dynamic client registration, PKCE; Settings › Connect Krea opens Krea's sign-in in a tab (in the app, the usual browser: the opener's capability allows only krea.ai URLs; with no URL scope every link was refused), Krea sends it back to `/api/krea-callback`; once connected the one button is Sign out of Krea; `start_login` / `finish_login` / `forget_login`); the client and its tokens live in `studio/.krea_auth.json` (gitignored, 0600, one sign-in per machine); the access token is refreshed before it expires and after a 401. Opencode is not used any more.
- `studio/qa_engine.py` — logo QA on generated plates (needs `demo-info/design-system/logo-cream.png`). `demo-info/` also holds the design-system PDFs and the playbook: reference, not read by code.
- `studio/chapters.py` (territories), `diversity.py` (random axes), `taste.py` (learning from kept/trashed), `critic.py` (optional AI scoring, off by default), `web/src/drone.js` (the synthesized drone, WebAudio), `sequences.py` (Manual/AI orders), `editor.py` + `features.py` (AI ordering), `bench.py` (scripted generation runs, optional).
- `studio/gen_log.py` — the written record of what the server did (the server's own output lines carry their time too) (`studio/generation_log.jsonl`, gitignored, rotates at 5 MB): server starts, Kimi batches, every image (all retries, logo check, seconds), AI orders, exports, deletions. Read it with `npm run log` (or `python3 studio/gen_log.py --failures`), or `GET /api/log`. An `image_start` with no result followed by a `server_start` means a restart cut the image short.
- `studio/curation_state.json` — DEMO's approvals, trash, order, titles, prompts, per-plate model. Committed on purpose: it is the selection history (other projects keep theirs in `projects/<slug>/state.json`).
- `assets/ai-plates/` — generated plates (committed). `outputs/*.mp4` is gitignored.

## Run
`README.md` is the guide for someone setting it up on their own computer (a colleague): `npm run setup` (`scripts/setup.sh`: checks Python 3.10+, Node 20+, ffmpeg and git-lfs, offers `brew install` for what is missing, `git lfs pull`, `.venv` + pip, `npm install`; safe to run again), then Settings › Connect Krea and the Kimi key (Generate opens Settings on whichever is missing). ffmpeg is found on PATH or Homebrew's places (`find_tool`).
```fish
npm install        # once
npm run dev        # API with auto-restart (studio/dev.py) + Vite with hot reload -> http://localhost:5173
```
Separately: `npm run api` (backend only, restarts when `studio/*.py` changes, the page reloads itself) and `npm run web` (Vite only). `npm run build` writes `studio/web/dist` and the backend then serves the page at :8765. Needs Python 3 with Pillow (`pip3 install -r requirements.txt`), ffmpeg at `/opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg`, Node 20+. The user runs these themselves; do not start or kill their server unless asked. Do not restart the API mid-generation.

## Safety (from a review of the app branch)
- Guardrails (from a second review, of jobs against actions): the curation (`state.json`) is written whole or not at all (`_write_atomic`: a temp file, then `os.replace`), and one that cannot be read is set aside as `*.damaged-<time>.json`, never written over (a half-read file used to be replaced by an empty curation). Every read-change-write of it holds `STATE_LOCK` (trash, Settings, a new image's entry, the critic); a slow call (Kimi writing scenes, the critic, the key check) works from a copy and merges into the state as it is then, only in the project it started in (`started_in`). New images are written whole (`.part`, then renamed) before they appear. A job's requests say their project (`X-Phosphene-Project`, `forProject`): a request for a project no longer open is refused (409 `project_changed`); a page whose project was changed elsewhere reloads into it (`loadState`). While a batch or an export runs the page holds a lease (`POST /api/busy`, `_LEASE`, renewed every 10 s, at most 60 s): `busy()` stays true in the gaps, so no switch, New, import or delete from another window or the Mac menu, and `dev.py` never restarts mid-job (it also waits when /api/busy does not answer). The page's own files likewise: while a job runs, a reload from the development server (an edit to the page) is held (`src/devguard.js`, `vite:beforeFullReload`) and done once the job ends: an edit mid-batch had reloaded the page, the images in flight lost it, and Resume asked for them twice. Selection / Trash switch at once from what the page has (`renderView`), then refresh. Files opened from the Mac menu or Finder while the app runs go through the page (`page_import` → `importProjectPath`, which waits for a job); a page not ready yet (loading, reloading) hands the path back (`import-later`), kept in `Opened` and given to the page when it says it is ready (`page-ready`, after its first load): it used to reload and drop the file; New project / Project settings (⌘N too) wait; Empty trash and Delete selected wait for an export (it reads the images as it goes). Export project… reads the curation under the lock.
- Image names become paths only through `plate_name` (server) and `_clean_state` (a received project's curation): a bare file name, never `../` or an absolute path, so Empty trash can never delete outside the project's plates (a crafted .phosphene listing `/Users/.../thesis.pdf` as trashed did, before).
- The server answers only requests to `localhost` / `127.0.0.1` by that name (`from_this_computer`: a domain pointed at 127.0.0.1, DNS rebinding, is refused) and takes commands (POST) only from its own page (an `Origin` that is not local is refused: another website cannot empty the trash or switch project). Clients with no Origin (the app shell, curl, tests) are local by construction.
- The static fallback serves only `/assets/` (the old plate URLs), never a dotfile: `studio/.secrets.json` and `.krea_auth.json` are not reachable.
- A damaged project never stops the server: `open_first_project` passes it over (the next project, else the first screen). Imports: numeric names are made text, Finder's `__MACOSX`/`._` entries are ignored, a damaged zip says so and leaves nothing behind, two imports at once cannot delete each other's folder. Switching project holds `STATE_LOCK`. Krea token refreshes are one at a time (`_REFRESH_LOCK`). In the app no folder is made inside Phosphene.app (it may run read-only from the DMG).
- The app's port is fixed (47615) when free: the Krea sign-in comes back to the same address and the page's remembered settings (tied to the address) survive a relaunch. The menu bar: About Phosphene (the icon, the version, one line on what it is: `AboutMetadata`), New Project ⌘N, Open Project File… ⌘O (the macOS file dialog), Export Project… ⌘E, Curate ⌘1, Film ⌘2, Settings ⌘,, the standard Edit menu (the page gets every shortcut first: its own ⌘Z / ⌘S / ⌘A are not doubled). Files opened from Finder are imported off the main thread.

## Batches survive the real world (studio/web/src/app.js `runBatch`)
A lost connection or Krea down (`offline` from /api/generate-image: 5xx gateway codes, URL / socket errors, or the page's request
throwing) pauses the batch ("Connection lost: waiting to carry on…") and `/api/net` (Krea reachable?) is asked every 5 s until it is
back, then it carries on by itself; no Krea credits (`stop`) stops it, and the images that hit the limit wait for Retry with the rest; an HTTP error is Krea answering, not a lost connection (only 502/503/504/52x are: a 401 / 403 / 404 read as offline was resubmitted forever, a paid job each time; a 401 says Connect Krea, `needs_auth`); an image still offline after 3 rounds while Krea answers fails on its own (Retry); an image failing on its own is kept for "Retry N" at the end
(same scenes, Kimi not asked again; `retryable` false for a moderation rejection); a moderation refusal (Krea said no twice, the second time to a softened prompt) is not a failure: nothing was made, so nothing goes to the Trash; its bar is grey (`blocked`) and it is counted on its own; the summary is one short line of counts (`Done · 1 added · 1 to Trash by the logo check · 1 refused by Krea's moderation`), each bar's hover gives that image's reason and the summary's hover all of them (`prog.detail`); Stop (in the status bar, it wakes a waiting batch) or
a reload, quit or crash keeps what was not made on this computer (`studioPending:<slug>`), offered when the page opens: "N images of
the last batch weren't made · Resume · Discard" (`offerPending`; images already made meanwhile, or still being made on the server (`making` in /api/state, `MAKING`), by title, are skipped; the server also refuses a second request for an image it is making, `duplicate`, 409, which the page counts as arriving by itself: a reload mid-batch then Resume had made three images twice). The status
bar's buttons are `prog.actions`. The Mac app asks before quitting or closing the window while a job runs (`page-busy` event from the
page, `PAGE_BUSY`, `ask_before_quitting`: "Quit anyway" / "Keep working").

## Secrets
- A key is checked when saved (`check_kimi_key`: each endpoint's `/models`): one that Kimi refuses everywhere is not kept (the page says Key refused and that keys start with sk-); the endpoint that took a key is remembered and tried first (`KIMI_ENDPOINT`, `kimi_endpoints`), so a call does not go round the others. The key field is a text field masked with `-webkit-text-security` (as a password field, macOS filled a saved password into it).
- The Kimi API key lives in `studio/.secrets.json` (gitignored) or `KIMI_API_KEY` / `MOONSHOT_API_KEY`. Never write it into `curation_state.json` or any committed file.

## Picture renderer (studio/web/src/render.js)
One engine for the picture, as `drone.js` is for the sound (merged from the `webgl-renderer` branch after the user judged it): a WebGL2 renderer (`window.Render.create(canvas)`, `draw({ cur, ghost })`, `loadImage`) draws the stage on `#stageCanvas` (`initGl` / `paintGl`; `applyFx`, `drawTrail` and `clearFx` set `glLook`; the page still chooses which image shows when, and `#stageImg` / `#stageGhost` only feed it, hidden by `body.gl`). Without WebGL2 the stage falls back to CSS (the node unit tests run that way, with a fake `Render` for the export), but there is no export: it says WebGL2 is off. The export draws every frame with the same renderer at 1080 × 1920 from the full-size plates (`exportFrames`: frame f shows the clip at f + 0.5), JPEG 0.92, and sends them to `POST /api/export-frame?i=` (frame 0 starts a new set; a held run goes as one request, `&same=k&to=j`, after frame k arrived, stored as hard links so a long film takes no room; kept in `studio/.export/frames`, at most `EXPORT_FRAMES_MAX`); `/api/export-video` with `frames: N` checks every frame is there and only encodes. The server no longer draws frames or plans the rhythm (Pillow's `write_export_frames` and `cutplan.py` are gone). The shader uses the old export's numbers (grey = 1 - Pillow's 601 luma, the trail a plain mix, zoom a crop of the centre 1/scale, images cover the frame, no colour management); e2e tests read the renderer's pixels and decode the exported frames (plates, inverted copies, the trail's fade). Python export tests go through `_export` (frames first, as the page does).

## Sound engine performance (studio/web/src/drone.js)
The live drone must never make the page janky. What keeps it cheap, and what tests guard (`studio/tests/e2e/drone.e2e.test.cjs`): cut-time events, hits and arpeggio notes are scheduled ~0.8 s ahead (`advance`, ticked every 90 ms), not for the whole clip; the arpeggio and each hit type are fixed voices retriggered by automation (no node per note); the reverb convolver is shared across swaps (loading its impulse response is the costliest step); noise and room sample data are made once per sample rate and shared by every context; settings changes are coalesced into one swap per ~120 ms, a swap whose only difference is where the clip ends (the Multiple) is skipped, and the exact loudness is measured when the page is idle. A new sound starts a moment ahead (the time a build takes), and its fade-in and the old sound's fade-out are timed from that instant: a fade timed before the build made every swap enter with a step. The room's impulse is noise band-limited to 150-900 Hz: low-passed noise alone drifts, and for some seeds made the room a strong boost near 100 Hz that swamped the sub (a test renders 20 seeds with a big room). Measured on a 300-cut fast clip with every layer on: a swap ~12 ms, longest main-thread block ~45 ms (it was 675 ms). WebKit (Safari, the Mac app) can stop a page's sound by itself while it plays (`interrupted` / `suspended`: another app's sound, an output change, sleep) and never start it again: `Live` asks it back at once (`onstatechange`, `_revive`), and again on focus, a click or a key, then the page puts it where the picture is (`onRevived` → `seek`). In the Mac app the sound also went silent after the window had been behind another app while the context still said `running`: in WebKit (`renewOnReturn`, Apple's `navigator.vendor`; Chrome keeps its sound), back in front while playing, `renew` closes the context and the page plays a fresh one from the picture's place (`onRenew` → `startMusic`; ~0.5 s gap). The console log (right-click › Inspect Element in `app:live`) showed worse: after a clack, every context, new ones too, said `running` and stayed silent; only a reload helped. So a watchdog (`_watch`, never a reload while a job runs: a fresh sound instead) checks every second, while the page is in front, that the sound's clock (`currentTime`) moves; two stopped seconds call `onStall`: a fresh sound, and stopped again within 20 s, a reload that plays on from the same place (`studioResume`). Every event is logged as `[sound hh:mm:ss]` (`soundLog`). The app also has `NSAppSleepDisabled` (app/src-tauri/Info.plist: no App Nap). The cause, found in another Tauri app on macOS 27 (thecrateapp/crate#270): hidden ~4 minutes, WKWebView suspends the page's process and deactivates its audio session; the context still says `running`, the output is dead until a reload. The window has `disable_drag_drop_handler()`: Tauri's file-drop handler took every drag, so cards could not be reordered in the app (the page's drag test passes in Chrome and WebKit; only the shell broke it; README's checklist has a drag). The window is made with `background_throttling(Disabled)` (WKInactiveSchedulingPolicy::None, macOS 14+): never suspended (timers keep their pace while hidden). `renewOnReturn` is off: rebuilding on return did not mend it and added a gap. By hand, the reset is Sound › Source Off then Synthesized (`updateMusic` → `Live.fresh`: a new context), no extra button (one control per feature); its tooltip says so. `renderExport` / `level` / `Live` accept any numbers (empty or one-cut timelines, NaN, huge offsets) without throwing.
